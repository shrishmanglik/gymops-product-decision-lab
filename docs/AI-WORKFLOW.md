# AI-assisted authoring workflow

## Truth boundary

AI assisted research synthesis, product framing, implementation drafting, test generation, and defect correction during repository authoring. The public product does not call a model at runtime. No measured speed, quality, adoption, or business result is claimed.

## Context packet

The authoring session was limited to:

- a normalized capture of a public Product Manager role brief;
- a read-only public application surface;
- a verified candidate record used only for claim ceilings outside this product;
- proven release-process requirements;
- repository-owned synthetic gym-operations signals;
- the constraint that real product behavior, customer evidence, and employer facts remain unknown.

No employer account, codebase, customer record, support ticket, product screenshot, private backlog, or application field was provided to the product builder.

## Workflow version `author-review-evaluate-1.0.0`

1. Derive one domain-specific governing constraint from the role evidence.
2. Separate observations, interpretations, and requested solutions.
3. Generate multiple interventions and force an explicit build, defer, and reject decision.
4. Draft the operating state machine and engineer-buildable contract.
5. Convert every requirement into a deterministic evaluator or an explicit unknown.
6. Create a known-bad and clean fixture for each evaluator.
7. Attack the controls by removing rules, disabling evaluators, substituting reason codes, corrupting digests, replaying receipts, self-approving, and generalizing across locales.
8. Run type, lint, test, build, dependency, browser, accessibility, viewport, contamination, and repeatability gates.
9. Route the exact source state to a distinct reviewer. The author does not review its own work.
10. Bind any public deployment to the independently approved commit and verify anonymously.

## Accepted suggestions

- Store observation separately from requested solution.
- Use a bounded timed-offer scenario because it crosses scheduling, attendance, membership, and communication surfaces.
- Make expiration, capacity, permission, eligibility, staff pause, and reset executable rather than descriptive.
- Bind each control identifier to one canonical failure reason outside the evaluator.

## Rejected suggestions

- Infer utilization, retention, revenue, or time-saved improvement from synthetic signals.
- Present a predictive attendance model without consent, history, bias evaluation, and monitoring evidence.
- Treat repository fixtures as customer findings.
- Claim that the sample reflects an employer's current workflow.
- Permit the builder to mark its own release approved.

## Evaluation boundary

AI output is not accepted because it is fluent. It advances only when:

- every public statement resolves to a permitted input, implementation proof, or explicit claim state;
- deterministic controls fail on their named bad fixtures and pass on clean counterparts;
- a disabled detector, wrong code, stale digest, or self-approval attempt is caught;
- a different session independently reviews the exact commit.

The seed AI receipt records permitted evidence identifiers, input/output digests, citation coverage, accepted and rejected suggestions, and human-review disposition. It is a workflow receipt, not proof of model productivity.
