# Evidence and source boundary

## Permitted

- Normalized public role evidence held by the governed application-package lane.
- Repository-owned synthetic signal, workflow, intervention, and waitlist fixtures.
- Local source, deterministic test output, build output, screenshots, and receipts from this repository.
- Provider and anonymous-live readback only after an independently approved commit is deployed.

## Absent and not inferred

- Employer code, architecture, product behavior, roadmap, strategy, metrics, customers, support records, usage records, and endorsement.
- Candidate answers concerning pay, availability, location, consent, qualifications, or application intent.
- A measured productivity improvement, business outcome, customer outcome, or product-market result.

## External action ledger

This local product build performs no application submission, upload, message, account action, product write, notification, or provider mutation. Git publication, review, deployment, and package relinking belong to a separate release lane with explicit evidence.
